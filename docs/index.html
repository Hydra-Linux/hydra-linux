⚡ Powered by flash

Custom source-based package manager with intelligent 3-tier builds. Small packages compile, medium packages download binaries, massive packages assemble from pre-compiled parts.

🎨 Choose your desktop

KDE Plasma 6 (default), GNOME, Xfce, or Sway — pick your environment during installation. All running on Wayland with PipeWire audio.

🔧 Source-first, smart defaults

Packages under 100MB build from source. 100–450MB use binaries. Over 450MB? Pre-compiled parts assemble locally. Best of both worlds.

How flash Handles Packages

When you run flash install <package>, it checks the source size and chooses the fastest strategy automatically.

≤ 100 MB

⚙️ Source Build

Packages under 100MB are compiled entirely from source inside a sandboxed bubblewrap environment. You get full optimization for your CPU with the exact flags you configure in flash.conf.

Download source→Verify GPG→Extract→Build in sandbox→Install
Examples: fastfetch, curl, vim, OpenRC, wayland, sway, wireguard-tools, most packages
100–450 MB

📦 Binary Download

Medium packages use pre-built binaries from the repository. No compilation — just download, verify, and install. This saves hours on large toolchains and browsers while keeping the experience fast.

Check repo→Download .tar.zst→Verify GPG→Extract to root→Run scripts
Examples: Firefox, LLVM/Clang, Rust, WebKitGTK, Chromium, Linux kernel
> 450 MB

🧩 Part Assembly

Massive packages are split into logical modules during build. Pre-compiled parts are downloaded and assembled locally — faster than a full source build, more flexible than a blob binary.

Discover parts→Download each .part.tar.zst→Verify signatures→Merge into assembly dir→Run linker→Install
Examples: Qt 6, KDE Plasma, GCC
